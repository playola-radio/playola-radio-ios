# iPhone Speaking Position Illustration

## Purpose

Create one editable instructional illustration on the existing `playola-ios.pen` canvas showing where a person should speak while recording with an upside-down iPhone.

## Composition

- Use a clean, flat visual treatment consistent with the existing Playola iOS design language.
- Show one vertically oriented iPhone rotated upside down.
- Place an imaginary microphone 3–4 inches above the phone, represented visually as approximately half the phone's length.
- Connect the phone and imaginary microphone with a restrained dotted distance guide.
- Make the imaginary microphone and the label `TALK HERE` the primary focal point.
- Add a secondary callout identifying the real microphone at the upward-facing end of the upside-down iPhone.

## Scope

- Add the illustration in unoccupied canvas space as a single top-level frame.
- Keep every element editable as native pen.dev layers.
- Do not redesign or modify existing application screens or reusable components.
- Do not add photorealistic imagery, custom-generated artwork, or unrelated instructional copy.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The phone's upside-down orientation is immediately understandable.
- The intended speaking location reads as roughly 3–4 inches, or half a phone-length, above the device.
- `TALK HERE` is legible and visually dominant.
- The real microphone location is separately and unambiguously labeled.
- The frame has no clipped, collapsed, misaligned, or low-contrast content.
