Flip to record
Tapping Record on any of the 7 recording screens asks the user to flip the phone and talk into the bottom mic.

Skipped when an external mic is active or the phone is already upside down.

Prompt direction A: bottom sheet over the recorder.
Prompt direction B: full-screen takeover with the speaking-position illustration.
